Career Command

My job-search hub inside Hermes — one master record of my experience that every résumé, cover letter and application is built from.

Built on Hermes · Built with Claude under my direction · Python, JavaScript

← All projects

What it does

  • One master record — filled from my documents and a guided interview — feeds every résumé, so one fix updates them all.
  • Tailor for a job: pick an industry and role, optionally paste the posting, and get an ATS-friendly résumé (Word and PDF) plus cover letters.
  • An application pipeline from saved job to interview, with follow-up dates sent to my calendar.
  • A truth check that flags broken links, dead pages and wrong details on job leads — flagged, never silently hidden.

Built for more than me

  • Each person gets their own private profile and job search; nothing bleeds between people.
  • Helpers can be given access, with limits the owner sets.
  • Built to work for job seekers in other countries too, starting with English, Filipino, Spanish, Portuguese and Cebuano.
