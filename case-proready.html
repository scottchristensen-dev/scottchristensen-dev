ProReady

A study platform for certification exams. Its first test run: a full CompTIA A+ course I built on it.

Built with Claude under my direction · Next.js, TypeScript, PostgreSQL, Prisma, systemd

← All projects

What it does

Multi-subject course packs, spaced-repetition review (FSRS), an instructor review queue and encrypted content storage.

First test run: a CompTIA A+ course

  • All 9 exam domains across Core 1 (220-1201: hardware, networking, mobile, cloud, troubleshooting) and Core 2 (220-1202: operating systems, security, software troubleshooting, procedures) plus two timed 90-question mock exams.
  • Every generated flashcard and question was read in review; about 5–8% were tossed, each with a logged reason.
  • Study kit: 28 diagrams drawn in code, 24 study games and captions on lesson videos.
  • One-tap “Next up” picks the next lesson, its game, due cards or missed questions.

Real problems it solved

  • A live outage while the health check stayed green: a test build had been written into the production folder. I restored service and approved two guards — separate test-build folders and a health check that verifies the build.
  • The backup restore test could pass with every course image missing. I rebuilt it to check every stored file and restore from the off-site copy.
  • An “Add image” button silently did nothing (a form nested inside a form) even though automated tests passed. A live click-through caught it; I fixed it and added a guard test.
  • On phones, every study guide scrolled sideways because a reader panel was fixed at about 815 px wide. Fixed so guides fit the screen.

Keeping it current

Upgraded to Prisma 7 and Next.js 16 on a separate branch and scratch database, compared integration results before and after — the same failing set, so no regression — then shipped with a passing health check.
